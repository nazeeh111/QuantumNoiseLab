# Dependencies

QuantumNoiseLab is an original application, not a renamed copy of QuTiP. Dependencies are installed separately and retain their distributions' license files and notices:

- [QuTiP](https://github.com/qutip/qutip): BSD-3-Clause numerical quantum dynamics.
- [NumPy](https://github.com/numpy/numpy): BSD-3-Clause arrays and random sampling.
- [SciPy](https://github.com/scipy/scipy): BSD-3-Clause scientific routines used by QuTiP.
- [Matplotlib](https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib): its PSF-derived license and bundled third-party notices cover plotting and fonts.
- Development tools pytest, build, and setuptools retain their own notices.

No third-party source, logos, or promotional images have been copied into this repository. Generated plots use local Matplotlib/DejaVu rendering; no downloaded imagery is required. Consult dependency distributions for their complete notices, including compiled numerical libraries.

Numerical API reference: [QuTiP dynamics and time evolution](https://qutip.readthedocs.io/en/stable/apidoc/solver.html). Research using QuTiP should follow its scientific citation guidance. The application does not claim authorship of the underlying solver.
